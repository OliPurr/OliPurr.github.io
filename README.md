# LovaldApps

Developer site of LovaldApps, publisher of Shakespurr: Cat Translator.

This repository serves the root of olipurr.github.io. It holds the files that app stores and advertising networks read at the domain root, such as app-ads.txt. The legal pages of the apps, privacy policies and terms, live in the repository lovaldapps and are served under olipurr.github.io/lovaldapps/.

Contact: through the developer address in the Google Play listing.
